# Research studies

Start with [the authoritative Phase 17 status](../../studies/phase17/reports/phase17_authoritative_status.md)
and [the defect ledger](../../studies/phase17/reports/phase17_harden_defect_ledger.md).
These distinguish current conclusions, superseded digits and unresolved limits.

## What “Phase 17” means

It is the historical research campaign namespace, not a software version.
Suffixes identify specific hypotheses, model checks or repairs. Reports retain
their original identities so that a later fix cannot silently rewrite the
evidence used by an earlier conclusion.

Use the [Phase 17 reproduction guide](../../studies/phase17/README.md) for scripts,
configurations, results and provenance. The [baseline research guide](../research/research-guide.md)
explains earlier estimator-comparison families.

## A safe reading order

1. Read the question, truth assumptions and external-data requirements.
2. Check whether its result is current in the authoritative status.
3. Inspect the saved configuration, seed and producing source identity.
4. Follow qualification and defect references before citing a number.

The quickstart gallery is a small onboarding example. It does not replace the
study record or imply that every research path has the same data requirements.
Large kernels and gravity fields remain external; see the
[data policy](../../data/README.md).
